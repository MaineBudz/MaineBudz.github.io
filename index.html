Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting Budz Emporium.

How much does cannabis cost at Budz Emporium? ↓

Pre-rolls start at $3.50 and whole ounces are $87.50. Weekly specials offer 10–20% off selected flower strains, edible bundles, and pre-roll packs. All everyday pricing is backed by our Best Value Guarantee.

What are your store and bistro hours? ↓

Budz Emporium and Budz Café & Bistro are open 7 AM to 7 PM every single day of the year—365 days a year with zero holiday closures!

Do you have food and coffee on site? ↓

Yes! Budz Bistro operates right inside our Medway dispensary, featuring hand-crafted hot and iced coffees, espresso, breakfast items, and our popular Budz Boosts with 50+ Red Bull flavors.

Who can shop at Budz Emporium? ↓

Our store is adult-use only. You must be 21 years or older with a valid state or government-issued photo ID. Please consume responsibly.
