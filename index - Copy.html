About Budz Emporium

Our Mission

At Budz Emporium, we are dedicated to providing the highest quality cannabis products crafted with care and expertise. As Maine's premier dispensary, we serve our community with integrity, knowledge, and an unwavering commitment to excellence.

Value You Can Trust

Budz Emporium is a cannabis dispensary in Medway, Maine. We are open 7 AM–7 PM every single day of the year — 365 days, with no holidays off — and our team is here to guide you through our selection of flower, edibles, concentrates, vapes and accessories.

Stop in for Budz Café and Budz Bistro while you shop, and buy with confidence thanks to our Best Value Guarantee.

Whether you're seeking medicinal relief or looking to indulge in recreational enjoyment, we offer a diverse range of products from flower to edibles, concentrates to accessories. All our products are tested and verified for safety and potency.
